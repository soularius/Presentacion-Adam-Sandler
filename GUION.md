## ✏️ GRAMÁTICA QUE USAMOS (para el punto "topics seen in class")

Cada diapositiva tiene una etiqueta pequeña ✏️ con el tema. Si el profe pregunta:

- **Verb to be + present simple** → *He is an actor. He makes people laugh.* (diap. 2, 10)
- **Past simple (regular e irregular)** → *was born, grew up, moved, started, became, fired, came back, wore, walked out* (diap. 3, 4, 5, 11)
- **Present perfect** → *He has made more than 50 movies. He has worked with the same friends for 30 years.* (diap. 6, 14)
- **Can / can't (habilidad)** → *He can't skate, but he can hit a golf ball very far.* (diap. 7)
- **Wh- questions** → *Which movie is it? Why did we choose him?* (diap. 3, 9)
- **Comparativos y superlativos** → *more serious than ever, the highest-paid actor, the most iconic* (diap. 6, 13)
- **Imperativos** → *Never give up! Be yourself!* (diap. 15)

> ⚠️ Si en clase vieron otros temas (por ejemplo *used to*, *going to*, *first conditional*), avísenme y los meto en el guion y en las etiquetas.
> Ideas rápidas: *"When he was a kid, he **used to** make his classmates laugh."* · *"If you watch Happy Gilmore, you **will** laugh a lot."*
